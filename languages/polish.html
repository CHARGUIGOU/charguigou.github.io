          TRIVIA
          • Disney and Poland signed a contract in 1958 for the release of Disney films in the country; however, this contract was canceled by Disney in 1969 after the release of The Sword in the Stone, when they discovered that the Polish distributor was delivering copies illegally to the Russian embassy in Warsaw, which were then distributed and shown throughout the USSR.
            Source : polski-dubbing.pl
            The Polish dubs only resumed in the early 90s after the fall of USSR.
