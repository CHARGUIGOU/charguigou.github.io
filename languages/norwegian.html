          TRIVIA
          • Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs was originally planned to be dubbed in Norwegian back in 1938, as Disney was touring Europe and the Middle East to sell the film. The idea of a Norwegian dub was eventually dropped.
            Source : The Pittsburgh Press 26-Feb-1938
          • Alice in Wonderland was the first Norwegian Disney dub to be released, but not the first to be made: Fun and Fancy Free was the first Disney movie to get a Norwegian version, with the process starting in 1950.
            Source : Newspaper 29-Jul-1950
          • However, Norway's initial dubbing spree was short-lived: it stopped after Peter Pan in 1953 and only resumed almost two decades later, with The Aristocats in 1971.
            The same would happen again after Robin Hood in 1974; until dubbing resumed permanently in 1982 with The Fox and the Hound. The reasons are unknown (possibly financial).
