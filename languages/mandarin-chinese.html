          TRIVIA
          • China has always controlled the importation of foreign films into its market, depending on the success of its own local productions and on political tensions with Hollywood.
            It might be because of this that Disney originally chose Taiwan for the production of their Chinese dubs in the early 1990s. However, Disney quickly tried out dubs made in Mainland China, with The Hunchback of Notre Dame being the first known case of a dub produced on the mainland (One Hundred and One Dalmatians might have been the first, for the 1995 cinema re-issue).
            It was not until Mulan in 1998 that two separate Mandarin dubs were produced, although China has been inconsistent over the years. It made its own dubs of several classics in the late 2000s/early 2010s, aimed at television releases.
