SDI Media Hong Kong Ltd is the <i>Hong Kong subsidiary</i> of SDI Media, specializing in dubbing and subtitling.
It was created on 3 September 1991 as <i>High Art Limited</i>, before changing to <i>Media Watchers Limited</i> in 1994, and <i><b>SDI Media Hong Kong Limited</b></i> in 2000 after being bought in 1998.
<br>It was eventually bought again in 2021 by <b>Iyuno Group</b> and changed its name in 2022. Therefore, it's still technically active, but under another name "<b><a href="/artists/iyuno-hk">Iyuno Media Hong Kong Limited</a></b>".
