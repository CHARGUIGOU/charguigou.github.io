  • The voices followed by a ? are the voices from the first movie who appears in the additional voices in this one. Nothing actually confirms they reprised their character(s); and they might have dubbed others as well.
  • The Disney+ credits actually mention more small additional roles of the star-talents.
  • In the Cantonese version :
    The voices of Snow White?, Anna, Elsa and Moana are from the original classic movies;
    The voice of Rapunzel? is from the 2017 TV-Series.
  • Tommy Chu (朱栢康), the new voice of Ralph, was the voice of Wynnchel and Zangief in the first movie. He didn't reprise his role as Wynnchel, dubbed instead here by star-talent Jason Chau (周世鏗).
  • "Melodee Mak" Mak6 Zi2 Gwan1 (麥紫筠), the new voice of Vanellope, was credited as 麥智鈞 (Mak6 Zi3 Gwan1). She was the voice of Taffyta Muttonfudge in the first film; but didn't even reprise her character, dubbed instead by star-talent Angela Yuen (袁澧林).
  • Several Youtubers were called to dub smaller and cameo characters :
    • Dou Siu-kiu (杜小喬), the voice of Little Debbie and Candlehead, is a Short Films Youtuber, also known by her stage name 杜以辰 (Dou6 Ji5 San4). She is now also an actress.
    • Ho Wing-syun (何穎璇), known as Miss Hunny, the voice of Tiffany Herrera and Jubileena Bing-Bing, is a Magician Youtuber, famous for being Hong Kong's first female magician YouTuber.
    • Jason Chau (周世鏗), also known as BigJ (大J), the voice of Butcher Boy, McNeely and Wynnchel, is a Gaming & Vlog Youtuber.
    • Edward Slime (屎萊姆), the voice of Pyro, Office Nerd Lee and Duncan, is also a Gaming Youtuber.
      Ching Ching (屎嫂程程), the voice of Mo's Mom, is his wife, and also a Family & Lifestyle Youtuber.
    • Chung Chung (蔥蔥), the voice of Josey, is a Gaming & Vlog Youtuber.
    • Cfun (司勳), credited as 司勲, the voice of the Auctioneer, is a Radio Emcee and content creator.
  • Peggy Kwok (郭碧珍), the dubbing director, died of an undisclosed illness on November 9, 2020, aged 49.
  • "Kitty Wong" Wong4 Zi2 Laam4 (黃紫藍), part of the additional voices - most certainly Elsa, among others - was credited with her real name, 黃紫嫻 (Wong4 Zi2 Haan4).
  • Can4 Juk1 Hang4 (陳旭恆) - additional voice - was credited as 陳旭恒. Pronunciation and romanization are the same.
