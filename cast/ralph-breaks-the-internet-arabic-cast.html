  • Release dates :
    Kuwait / Lebanon / Saudi Arabia - 22 November 2018 (Cinemas) (English dub)
    MENA - 6 March 2019 (iTunes) (Arabic dub)
  • As other movies between 2016 and 2022, the dialect of the film is hybrid: a mix of Egyptian Arabic and Modern Standard Arabic. While most of the film is in MSA, some lines (mainly jokes), the whole Princess scene and the songs are in Egyptian Arabic.
  • In the Arabic version :
    The voices of Tiana, Rapunzel, Anna, Elsa and Moana are from the original classic movies;
    The voice of Snow White started dubbing the character since "House of Mouse" in 2001;
    The voice of Jasmine is from the JeemTV dubs;
    The voices of Cinderella, Aurora, Ariel, Belle, Pocahontas, Mulan and Merida are new.
  • Rana Adel (رنا عادل), the singing voice of Ariel, will eventually dub the character in the 2023 live-action remake.
  • Ilham Sabry (إلهام صبري), the voice of Moana, was credited as Luma Sabry (لوما صبري).
