  • Release dates :
    Kuwait / Lebanon / Saudi Arabia - 22 November 2018 (Cinemas) (English dub)
    MENA - 6 March 2019 (iTunes) (Arabic dub)
  • As other movies between 2016 and 2022, the dialect of the film is hybrid: a mix of Egyptian Arabic and Modern Standard Arabic. While most of the film is in MSA, some lines (mainly jokes), the whole Princess scene and the songs are in Egyptian Arabic.
  • In the Arabic version :
    The voices of Tiana, Rapunzel, Anna, Elsa and Moana are from the original classic movies;
    The voice of Snow White started dubbing the character since "House of Mouse" in 2001;
    The voice of Jasmine is from the JeemTV dubs;
    The voices of Cinderella, Aurora, Ariel, Belle, Pocahontas, Mulan and Merida are new.
  • Kacau Gomes, the Brazilian voice of both Mulan and Tiana, was only allowed to dub one of them and so was asked to choose between the two.
    In the meantime, Sílvia Goiabeira, the original Brazilian voice of Jasmine, wasn't even called back to audition.
    Source : Interview with SANA
  • Nandu Valverde, the musical director, died on December 29, 2020 from cardiac arrest after being hospitalized for 48 days and undergoing surgeries for a ruptured abdominal hernia. He was 44 years old.
    Source : Diário do Grande ABC
  • Diego Marques, the voice of eBoy and Rancis, is the nephew of Jussara Marques, the voice of Tiffany Herrera and the Instagram Pop-Up.
  • Gabriela Milani was credited with her nickname Gaby Milani for her role as Aurora.
