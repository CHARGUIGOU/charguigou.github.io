DISNEY CHARACTER VOICES INTERNATIONAL, INC.
  • Release date :
    Brazil - 3 January 2019 (Cinemas)
  • In the Brazilian Portuguese version :
    The voices of Ariel, Pocahontas, Mulan, Rapunzel, Merida, Anna, Elsa and Moana are from the original classic movies;
    The voice of Jasmine and Belle are from the live-action remakes;
    The voices of Snow White, Cinderella, Jasmine and Tiana are new.
  • Natali Pazete, the voice of Cinderella in the 2015 live-action remake, didn't return for the character but dubbed Tiana instead.
  • Kacau Gomes, the Brazilian voice of both Mulan and Tiana, was only allowed to dub one of them and so was asked to choose between the two.
    In the meantime, Sílvia Goiabeira, the original Brazilian voice of Jasmine, re-adutioned for the role but wasn't selected. She said she did not understand why as she had recorded some speaking parts as Jasmine for Disney On Ice earlier that same year.
    Source : Interview with SANA
  • Nandu Valverde, the musical director, died on December 29, 2020 from cardiac arrest after being hospitalized for 48 days and undergoing surgeries for a ruptured abdominal hernia. He was 44 years old.
    Source : Diário do Grande ABC
  • Diego Marques, the voice of eBoy and Rancis, is the nephew of Jussara Marques, the voice of Tiffany Herrera and the Instagram Pop-Up.
