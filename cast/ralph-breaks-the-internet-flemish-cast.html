DISNEY CHARACTER VOICES INTERNATIONAL, INC.
  • Release date :
    Belgium - 12 December 2018 (Cinemas)
  • Belgium kept the original English title for the release.
  • In the Flemish version :
    The voices of Mulan, Rapunzel, Merida, Anna, Elsa and Moana are from the original classic movies;
    The voice of Belle is from the live-action remake;
    The voices of Snow White, Cinderella, Aurora, Ariel, Jasmine and Pocahontas are new, as their original films weren't dubbed in Flemish.
    The voice of Tiana is new as well.
  • Dorothee Dauwe, the voice of the Show Host, is a real life Radio Presenter.
  • Similarly, Linde Merckpoel, the voice of the News Anchor, is a Radio & Television Presenter.
