DISNEY CHARACTER VOICES INTERNATIONAL, INC.
  • Release date :
    Greece - 22 November 2018 (Cinemas)
  • In the Greek version :
    The voices of Aurora, Jasmine, Pocahontas, Mulan, Tiana, Merida, Anna, Elsa and Moana are from the original classic movies;
    The voices of Cinderella and Belle are from the live-action remakes;
    The voice of Rapunzel is from the TV series;
    The voices of Snow White and Ariel are new.
  • Andréas Evaggelátos (Ανδρέας Ευαγγελάτος), the voice of KnowsMore, was the voice of King Candy in the first film.
  • Ilías Zervós (Ηλίας Ζερβός), who was the voice of Gene in the first movie, might have reprised his character in this sequel, but also dubbed Double Dan.
  • Vasílis Mílios (Βασίλης Μήλιος) replaces Kóstas Tsákonas (Κώστας Τσάκωνας) as the voice of Mr. Litwak after the latter's death in 2015.
  • Iríni Aspióti (Ειρήνη Ασπιώτη), the voice of Snow White, was credited with her other name, Iríni Tsíga (Ειρήνη Τσίγκα).
