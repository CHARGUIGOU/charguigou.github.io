DISNEY CHARACTER VOICES INTERNATIONAL, INC.
  • Release date :
    Spain - 5 December 2018 (Cinemas)
  • In the Castilian Spanish version :
    The voices of Snow White, Cinderella, Ariel, Belle, Jasmine, Pocahontas, Mulan, Tiana, Rapunzel, Merida, Anna, Elsa and Moana are from the original classic movies;
    The voice of Aurora is new.
  • According to the site eldoblaje, Sam Dávila, the voice of Gene, was miscredited as José Antonio Dávila.
  • Some Youtubers were called to dub smaller and cameo characters :
    • Luna Dangelis is a Gaming Youtuber.
    • Strip Marvel is a Marvel related Youtuber.
    • Porexpan is a Harry Potter related Youtuber.
  • Salvador Aldeguer, the voice of Ralph, was credited with his nickname Patxi Aldeguer.
  • Pilar Martín, the voice of Taffyta, was credited as Piluca Martín.
  • Milagros Fernández, the voice of Jimmy's Grandma and the Instagram Pop-Up, was credited with her full name for the role of Jimmy's Grandma, María del Milagro Fernández.
