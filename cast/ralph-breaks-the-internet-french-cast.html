DISNEY CHARACTER VOICES INTERNATIONAL, INC.
  • Release dates :
    France - 19 January 2019 (L'Alpe d'Huez Film Festival)
    France - 13 February 2019 (Cinemas)
  • In the French version :
    The voices of Snow White, Ariel, Pocahontas, Mulan, Rapunzel, Merida, Anna, Elsa and Moana are from the original classic movies;
    The voices of Cinderella and Aurora are from the sequels;
    The voice of Belle is from the live-action remake;
    The voices of Jasmine and Tiana are new.
  • Marie Galey, who's been the singing voice of Ariel since 1998, reprised the speaking parts of the character in this movie.
  • Benoît Brière, the Quebec actor who voices here KnowsMore, was the voice of King Candy in the first film, for both France and Quebec again.
  • The performance of Olivier Benard as the Goat was used as the International version for most international dubs around the world.
  • Jean-Claude Donda, the voice of Grumpy (among others), was the voice of Doc in the 2001 re-dub of Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.
  • Following the original version, the voice of Baby Mo is done by Mila Pointet, the original voice of Toddler Moana in Disney's Moana (2016).
    She's the daugther of Dorothée Pousseo, the voice of Vanellope.
  • All the French additional voices, except My Abderrahim El Boumeshouli and Sylvain Urban, then performed the additional voices for the International version: a group of undistinct background voices (walla) in no recognizable language.
  • Denise Metmer, the voice of Jimmy's Grandma, was miscredited as Denis Metmer.
