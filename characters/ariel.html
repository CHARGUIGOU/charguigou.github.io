Ariel

Appearances

    The Little Mermaid (1989)

    Trivia

    • In the Brazilian, Danish and Japanese dubs, only the songs were redubbed in the 1997/1998 reissue - the rest of the dubs were kept as they were (Only Flounder's was voice also redubbed in the Danish version).
    • Sissel Kyrkjebø, a Norwegian singer, dubbed Ariel in three different versions, as a package for the Scandinavian dubs : in Danish for the singing voice, and in Norwegian and Swedish for both speaking and singing.
    • In the Cantonese dub, Cally Kwong (鄺美雲), the singing voice of Ariel, also sang a pop version of "Part Of Your World" in the end credits.
    • Simona Patitucci, the Italian voice of Ariel, won a prize as Best European Ariel in 2001. She should have gone to New York, but because of the terrorist attacks of 9/11/01, she couldn't collect it in person.
      Source : Simona Patitucci
    • Svetlana Svetikova (Светлана Светикова), the Russian voice, also won a prize for Best Foreign Version.
    • Valgerður Guðnadóttir didn't have to audition for the role. The directors only sent a demo of her singing and she got the part.
      Source : Valgerður Guðnadóttir
    • Martina Sprangers, the voice of Ariel in the first Thai dub (1991), was known as Martina Antoinette Jalleh. She's Malaysian, and adopted later the Thai nationality. As she was not fluent in Thai at the time of the dubbing, she had to memorize the dialogues from someone's else speaking, before reciting herself the lines.
      Source : Martina Sprangers
    • The Albanian dubs aren't official.

    The Little Mermaid - TV Series (1992-1994)

    Trivia

    • In the Finnish dub, Nina Tapio got to dub Ariel in one episode, before becoming the Finnish voice of the character in th 1998 redub.
    • In the Kazakh dub, Ariel's singing parts are sung by Ainūr Bermūhambetova (Айнұр Бермұхамбетова), who was the voice of Ursula in the animated movie.
    • In the Taiwanese Mandarin dub, the songs "Edge of the Sea" and "Harmony" were left in English on Disney+
    • In the Latin American Spanish dub, Rocío Robledo and Erika Robledo, the two speaking voices of Ariel, are sisters.
    • When the DVD "Disney Princess Party - Volume 1" (2004), including S01xE06 "Message in a Bottle" of the series, was released in Greece, the episode was redubbed for the occasion. This version was only used then.
    • In Iceland, only some episodes were dubbed for a VHS release. The full series was never fully dubbed.
    • In Poland, a VHS with the first 4 episodes of the series was released in 1996, using the same cast as the 1995 TV dub, but with new translation, direction and mixing.
    • It is unconfirmed whether the series was fully dubbed into Bulgarian, or if only some episodes were for some VHS releases. (You've got information?)
