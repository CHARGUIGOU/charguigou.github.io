Aurora also known as "Briar Rose"

Appearances

    Sleeping Beauty (1959)

    Storybooks & LPs

    Trivia

    • In the Danish dub, Ellen Winther Lembourn was originally chosen as both speaking and singing voice of Aurora. But during the recordings, it was noted that the singing voice should sound "smaller" ("mindre" stemme), and the choice fell on Inge Stauss.
    • In the 1959 LA Spanish dub, Estrellita Díaz was also originally supposed to provide both speaking and singing voice for Aurora. But she died before she could record the songs due to cardiovascular complication, aged only 33 years-old.
      Source : Tómbola Disney
    • Mariam Al-Khosht (مريم الخشت) was originally chosen as both speaking and singing of Aurora in the 2022 Modern Standard Arabic dub of the movie, but eventually didn't have the time to record the songs.
    • Daryna Murashchenko (Дарина Муращенко) was only 17 years-old when she dubbed Aurora in the Ukrainian dub.
    • The 2001 Spanish dub underwent some modifications in 2008 on some dialogues of the movie, especially Maleficent's. All the 2001 voices returned.
      Source : Doblaje Wiki
    • The Albanian dub isn't official.

    Disney Princess Enchanted Tales: Follow Your Dreams (2007)

    Trivia

    • While this sequel has a Quebec French dub, since the original movie "Sleeping Beauty" wasn't dubbed in Quebec, they used the French version for Aurora's short, dubbing only Jasmine's short.
    • The Albanian dub was licensed.

    Sofia the First: Holiday in Enchancia (2013)

    From Sleeping Beauty (1959) :
    Japanese (1995 re-dub), Portuguese (2008 dub), Romanian (2008 dub), LA Spanish (2001 dub)

    From Disney Princess Enchanted Tales (2007) :
    German, Norwegian, Castilian Spanish

    New voices :
    Arabic, Croatian, English, Greek (Alice), Korean, Polish (Mary Poppins), Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Serbian, Slovene, Turkish

    Trivia

    • Stefanía Hoúyia (Στεφανία Χούγια), the Greek voice, was also the voice of Sofia in the series, and dubbed Princesses Snow White and Ariel as well.
    • Jeong Hyeon-gyeong (정현경), the Korean voice, also dubbed Snow White, Cinderella and Jasmine in the series.
    • Marija Veljković (Марија Вељковић), the Serbian voice, also dubbed Snow White and Belle in the series.
    • The Albanian dub was licensed.

    Maleficent (2014)

    Portrayed by Elle Fanning

    Trivia
