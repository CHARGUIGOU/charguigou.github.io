    Aladdin (1992)

    Trivia

    • Karine Costa, the French singing voice of Jasmine, was only 15 years old when she sang for the character. She was the niece of the main Musical director, Georges Costa. She asked her uncle if she could audition for the singing parts, and eventually got the role.
    • Sílvia Goiabeira, the Brazilian Portuguese speaking voice, mentions she couldn't really enjoy the Premiere in São Paulo in 1993 because of sickness, which she would learn later was because of her unknown pregnancy.
      Source : SanaTV
    • Alexandra Wilcke (the voice of Nala and Pocahontas among others) auditioned on the song A Whole New World when she tried to enter Disney's dubs. Her performance was so appreciated by the studio that they tried to send it to the US; but the German casting had already been done and approved.
      Source : Alexandra Wilcke
    • Jannike Kruse, the Norwegian voice, was barely 18 years old when she dubbed the character.
    • Figen Sümeli and Otilia Maria Radulescu İpek, the Turkish voices, had previously dubbed Princess Belle together.
    • The Albanian dub isn't official.

    The Return of Jafar (1994)

    Trivia

    • Dubbed a decade later in Czech and after the third film, Sabina Laurinová eventually reprised the singing parts of the character in this sequel.
    • Ilia Swainson, the Danish voice, also reprised the speaking parts from this sequel onward.
    • While Quebec dubbed the first film, and would later dub the third one, this sequel was never dubbed - they use the European French dub instead.
    • The Albanian dub isn't official.

    Disney Princess Enchanted Tales: Follow Your Dreams (2007)

    Trivia

    • In the Arabic dub, while the dialogues were recorded in Jordan, the songs were recorded in Syria.
    • This is the only time Marta Barbarà didn't dub animated Jasmine in the Castilian Spanish dub.
    • In the Hebrew version, Meshi Kleinstein (משי קלינשטיין) would eventually reprise the role of Jasmine in the 2019 live-action remake.
    • Maggie Vera, the LA Spanish voice, eventually reprised the singing parts from this sequel onward.
    • The Albanian dub was licensed.

    Aladdin (Live-action) (2019)

    Portrayed by Naomi Scott

    Trivia

    • As for previous films, the European French version was kept in the Quebec French dub. However, as Quebec had different lyrics for the songs, Hiba Tawaji sang the song again with Quebec's lyrics. On the other hand, the songs Speechless is the same recording for both France and Quebec.
    • Lara Suleiman, the Brazilian voice, first passed the speaking audition and got accepted right away; but her audition for the singing parts was first rejected by Disney, thinking she wouldn't be able to cope with the song "Speechless". She had to record more of the song to show she could, and eventually got accepted for the singing as well.
      Source : Lara Suleiman
    • Marsha Songcome, the Swedish voice, had previously portrayed Jasmine in the West End Musical in 2016-2017.
    • Irina Arsenijević (Ирина Арсенијевић), the Serbian singing voice, was only 16 years old when she sang for the character. She had also dubbed the speaking line of Jasmine in the Serbian Trailer.
