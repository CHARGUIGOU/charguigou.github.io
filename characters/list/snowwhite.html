    Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937)

    Radio Plays & LPs

    Anecdotes

    • Frieda van Hessen, the Dutch 1938 singing voice of Snow White, was forced into hiding during WW2, and survived the Holocaust. She wrote a book about her life "Life in the Shadow of the Swastika".
    • Tatiana Angelini, the Swedish 1938 version, was Walt Disney's personal favourite foreign voice.
    • In 1996, Lucie Dolène (Snow White French 1962 voice) sued Disney France for the copyrights on her voice and won, leading to a complete redubbing of the movie in 2001.
    • Ratiba Al-Hefny (رتيبة الحفنى) was originally chosen by Disney to be the first song director in the history of Walt Disney's Arabic dubbings. Lots of girls were refused by Disney for the role of Snow White, so Ratiba then decided to record herself and send it to the Company under a nickname. To everyone's surprise, Disney liked it and chose her.
    • The second Thai dub is only a partial re-dubbing. Only Snow White, the Prince, and Grumpy were redubbed.
    • The Brazilian Portuguese (1938) and Japanese (1957) dubs are lost now.
    • The Albanian dubs aren't officially Disney's.

    LEGO Disney Princess: The Castle Quest (2023)

    Anecdotes

    • Despite not reprising the role in the Disney+ Modern Standard Arabic re-dub of the original movie in 2023, Noha Qais (نهى قيس) has been the new voice of Snow White since 2001 in House of Mouse and all her following appearances.

    Once Upon a Studio (2023)

    Anecdotes

    • Snow White only sings one line in this short - the voices here are all only singing.
    • Voices who returned from the original movie: French (2001 re-dub); Polish (2009 re-dub); Romanian (2009 dub); Serbian (2019 dub).
    • Voices who returned from the sequels or TV series: Dutch; Slovak (LEGO).
    • New voices: English; German; Hebrew.
    • In Slovak and Turkish, Natália Kóšová and Selen Severcan would later dub Rachel Zegler as Snow White in the 2025 live-action remake.

    Snow White (Live-action) (2025)

    Portrayed by: Rachel Zegler

    Anecdotes

    • Natália Kóšová, the Slovak voice, and Selen Severcan, the Turkish voice, reprised the role of Snow White whom they already dubbed in Once Upon a Studio. Natália Kóšová had also dubbed the character in LEGO Disney Princess.
