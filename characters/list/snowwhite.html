    Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937)

    Radio Plays & LPs

    Anecdotes

    • Frieda van Hessen, the Dutch 1938 singing voice of Snow White, was forced into hiding during WW2, and survived the Holocaust. She wrote a book about her life "Life in the Shadow of the Swastika".
    • Tatiana Angelini, the Swedish 1938 version, was Walt Disney's personal favourite foreign voice.
    • In 1996, Lucie Dolène (Snow White French 1962 voice) sued Disney France for the copyrights on her voice and won, leading to a complete redubbing of the movie in 2001.
    • Ratiba Al-Hefny (رتيبة الحفنى) was originally chosen by Disney to be the first song director in the history of Walt Disney's Arabic dubbings. Lots of girls were refused by Disney for the role of Snow White, so Ratiba then decided to record herself and send it to the Company under a nickname. To everyone's surprise, Disney liked it and chose her.
      Ratiba didn't want her real name to be written on the movie's opening, and asked for a nickname to be shown instead. However, after some years, she told her friends the truth.
    • The second Thai dub is only a partial re-dubbing. Only Snow White, the Prince, and Grumpy were redubbed.
    • The Brazilian Portuguese (1938) and Japanese (1957) dubs are lost now.
    • The Albanian dubs aren't official.

    LEGO Disney Princess: Villains Unite (2025)

    Anecdotes

    • In the Mandarin version, Mù Xuān-Míng (穆宣名) also dubbed Rapunzel.
    • In the Portuguese version, even though Sandra de Castro returned for Snow White in the first LEGO short in 2023, she didn't reprise the role in this one and only dubbed instead Cinderella.

    • This is the first LEGO Disney Princess short to be dubbed in Cantonese, Indonesian, Malay, Taiwanese Mandarin and Thai.
