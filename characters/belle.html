    Beauty and the Beast (1991)

    Trivia

    • Madhura Kumbhar (मधुरा कुंभार), the Marathi singing voice of Belle, would return in 2017 as Belle's singing voice in the Hindi version.
    • Jolanta Wilk, the Polish 2002 speaking voice of Belle, told in an interview for WIDZĘ GŁOSY that she first tried to model her voice after Paige O'Hara's, speaking in a slightly lower tone. But in the end Disney preferred her natural voice recording.
    • Alina Eremia, the Romanian voice, was only 17 years old when she dubbed the character.
    • Nguyễn Duyên Quỳnh, the Vietnamese voice, dubbed both the animated classic and live-action remake of Beauty and the Beast the same year.
    • The MS Arabic TV dub never aired.
    • The Albanian dubs aren't official.

    Beauty and the Beast: The Enchanted Christmas (1997)

    Trivia

    • The song "As Long as There's Christmas" was dubbed for a special Bulgarian Christmas VHS "Disney Winter Wonderland" (2003?). Yordanka Ilova (Йорданка Илова), the voice of Belle in the first film, sang for the character again.
    • In the Egyptian Arabic dub, the singing voice of Belle in the first film Dalia Farouq (داليا فاروق) eventually reprised the speaking parts of the character.
    • The Albanian dub isn't official.

    Beauty and the Beast (Live-action) (2017)

    Portrayed by Emma Watson

    Trivia

    • Alžbeta Bartošová, the Slovak voice, reprised the role she already dubbed in the animated film.
    • Madhura Kumbhar (मधुरा कुंभार), the Hindi singing voice of Belle, previously sang for the character in the Marathi dub of the animated film.
    • Nguyễn Duyên Quỳnh, the Vietnamese voice, dubbed both the animated classic and live-action remake of Beauty and the Beast the same year.
    • Anežka Pohorská, the Czech speaking voice, and Gabrielle Pietermann, the German speaking voice, are the regular voices of Emma Watson in their respective countries.
    • Michelle Jenner, the Castilian Spanish speaking voice, had also dubbed Emma Watson in the first four Harry Potter movies.
      She previously dubbed Chip in Beauty and the Beast: The Enchanted Christmas (1998) as well.
