    Cinderella (1950)

    Trivia

    • To find the original Latin American Spanish voice of Cinderella, the main TV station of Mexico City held a competition. Eventually Evangelina Elizondo was declared the winner.
    • The Icelandic dub has two different singers for Cinderella. Vigdís Pálsdóttir was originally chosen to be both the speaking and singing voice of Cinderella, but Disney USA eventually found her voice was too young-sounding in the singing parts, compared to the original dub. Þórunn Lárusdóttir was then chosen to be the singing voice, but Disney USA wasn't still happy with the result.
      The only compromise they found was to keep Vigdís Pálsdóttir in the first song, and Þórunn Lárusdóttir for the rest of the songs.
    • In the Vietnamese dub, Nguyễn Duyên Quỳnh dubbed both the animated film and the live-action remake the same year.
    • The Albanian dubs aren't official.

    Trivia

    • Croatia, Serbia and Slovenia dubbed the series, but not this pilot episode.
    • Thailand only dubbed the songs of the pilot episode "Once Upon A Princess" with Cinderella. The rest of the songs were left in English.
    • In the Dutch dub, although she didn't return in the sequels, Joke de Kruijf eventually reprised the role in this series.
    • In the Italian dub, after dubbing the character in the sequels, Monica Ward eventually reprised the singing parts as well.
    • On the opposite, in the Polish and Turkish dubs, Weronika Bochat and Tülay Uyar reprised the speaking parts of the character after only being the singing voice in the animated movie.
    • In the Romanian dub, Mediana Vlad eventually reprised the speaking parts of the character as well.
    • In the Slovak dub, dubbed after the live-action movie, Bianka Bucková returned for Cinderella.
    • The Albanian dub was licensed.
